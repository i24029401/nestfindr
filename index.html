05 - Safety

Six rules before you pay a single sen

Second-hand deals attract scammers. Follow these rules on NestFindr - and anywhere else.

  • 1. Never pay before viewing. No agent on NestFindr may ask for a deposit before a physical viewing.
  • 2. Verify the title. Ask for the land title and check the owner's name matches the seller's ID.
  • 3. Use our in-platform chat. Keep conversations on NestFindr until the viewing is booked.
  • 4. Check the renovation record. Major works need council approval - ask for the permits.
  • 5. Bring a second opinion. Hire an independent inspector before signing anything.
  • 6. Report suspicious listings. One click on "Report" - we review within 24 hours.
